WELCOME TO JO'S BAKERY

At Jo's Bakery, we believe every day should start with the smell of fresh bread.

WHO WE ARE

Jo's Bakery is a family-owned bakery based in Pretoria. Founded in 2025 by Jo Nkosi, what started as a small kitchen baking for neighbours during lockdown has grown into a trusted local bakery loved for quality, freshness, and community spirit.

We specialize in artisan breads, custom cakes, and pastries made from locally sourced ingredients.

WHY CHOOSE US

  • 100% Fresh daily - baked at 4am every morning
  • Family recipes passed down for 3 generations
  • Custom orders for birthdays, weddings, and events
